## What is Netuno?

It is a *low-code*, *open source* and completely free platform.

Our main goal is to boost your programming career by providing independence and total autonomy through mastering *fullstack* development.

The platform promotes *fullstack* development by demystifying the *backend*. It significantly simplifies several layers in the development of:

* Web solutions and applications;
* *Backoffice* systems;
* REST APIs.

By understanding the *fullstack* ecosystem, you will discover whether your natural aptitude leans more toward the *frontend* or the *backend*. You will also become a professional who is far better equipped to interact, communicate and collaborate with teams from different specialties.

## Prerequisites to get started

With Netuno, the learning curve is eased by removing several common technical barriers, but the human factor — your creativity and intelligence — remains essential.

Before starting your studies, it is essential to align your expectations and ensure that you have:

* **Willingness to leave your comfort zone:** Continuous learning is part of the journey in technology.
* **Interest and patience:** Learning new technologies and concepts requires time, practice and dedication.
* **Focus and persistence:** Software development requires a high level of concentration. Challenges will arise, but persistence is the key to success.
* **Desire for optimization:** A willingness to produce robust solutions faster and more efficiently.

:::tip Tips and Best Practices
* **Do not give up in the face of difficulties:** It is common for learning to develop your resilience indirectly. Many people give up when they encounter obstacles, but remember: if software development were extremely easy, it would already have been fully automated. Your human reasoning is indispensable.
* **Ask for help whenever you need it:** Move forward with confidence, but know that it is normal to feel lost at times along the journey. When that happens, do not hesitate to seek support.
:::


The platform is made for you to build in an agile and intelligent way.

You **will not need to spend money** to learn or use the technologies promoted and taught by Netuno. Our entire ecosystem and the tools we recommend are free.
