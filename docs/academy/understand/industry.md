## What is it?

Industry 4.0 is the most recent notion of industry, integrating the main technological innovations in the areas of automation, control and information in manufacturing.

The trend in production processes is for them to become increasingly independent, efficient and adaptable, through Cyber-Physical Systems, the Internet of Things and the Internet of Services.

This means a new era in the context of industrial revolutions. The way products are manufactured will change, thus affecting different areas of the market.

![Evolution of Industry](/docs/assets/business/industry-diagram-1.jpg "Evolution of Industry.")

The main principle of Industry 4.0 is that by interconnecting machines and systems, companies can create intelligent networks that control production parameters independently.

For example, scheduling maintenance, predicting failures or adapting to unplanned changes in production.

## Principles

Intelligent production systems that tend to emerge in the future through Industry 4.0 are defined by these 6 principles:

* **Real-time operation**: The ability to operate in real time translates into the acquisition and processing of data almost immediately, enabling real-time decision-making.
* **Virtualization**: Simulations are already used today, as are supervisory systems. However, Industry 4.0 proposes the existence of a virtual copy of smart factories, allowing remote monitoring of all their processes through sensors spread across the plant.
* **Decentralization**: The system can make decisions according to real-time production needs, receive commands and provide information about its work cycle.
* **Service orientation**: Use of service-oriented software architectures combined with the Internet of Services concept.
* **Modularity**: Production according to demand, coupling and decoupling of modules in production, offering flexibility to change machine tasks.

![Industry 4.0 Flow](/docs/assets/business/industry-diagram-2.jpg "Industry 4.0 Flow")

## What it is based on

Based on the principles above, Industry 4.0 is a reality made possible by the technological advances of the last decade, combined with technologies under development in the fields of information technology and engineering.

These are some of the most relevant technologies:

* **Internet of Things**: Consists of networking physical objects, environments, vehicles and machines through implemented electronic devices that allow data collection and exchange. Systems based on the Internet of Things are called cyber-physical and have sensors and actuators.
* **Big Data Analytics**: Very large and complex data structures that use new approaches for capturing, analyzing and managing information. Applied to Industry 4.0, Big Data technology consists of 6 C’s to handle relevant information:
  Connection, to the industrial network, sensors and PLCs; Cloud (cloud/on-demand data); Cyber (model and memory); Content; Community (share information); Customization (personalization and values).
* **Security**: The security and robustness of information systems is the great challenge for the success of Industry 4.0. Transmission failures in communication between machines or a simple system bug can disrupt production.

## Impacts

Industry 4.0 will undoubtedly affect the entire market, creating new business models.

In an increasingly demanding market, most companies seek to integrate individual customer needs and preferences into the product, avoiding prior customization of the product by consumers, thus facilitating the manufacturing process.

Research and development in the areas of information technology security, production reliability and machine interaction will be another aspect affected by Industry 4.0. This is because technology must develop continuously in order to make it possible for companies to adapt to this new industry standard.

Professionals will also be affected, since the replacement of manual work by automated labor is growing. In addition, they will need to adjust, because with more automated factories, new demands will arise and others will cease to exist.

On the other hand, demand in research and development will offer opportunities for technically skilled professionals with multidisciplinary training to understand and work with the variety of technologies that make up a smart factory.

## i4.0 Program

The Industry 4.0 Program is an initiative of the Portuguese Government that aims to accelerate the implementation of Industry 4.0 in Portugal.

This program has 3 main objectives:

##### 1. Accelerate the adoption of i4.0 by the Portuguese business fabric

Provide the business sector with knowledge and information

Promote a set of tools for business transformation

Train and readjust the national workforce

##### 2. Promote Portuguese technology suppliers as i4.0 players

Capitalize on the scientific and technological ecosystem

Create a favorable context for the development of i4.0 startups

Promote national technological solutions in an international context

##### 3. Make Portugal an attractive hub for investment in i4.0

Create favorable conditions (legal and tax) for investment targeted at Industry 4.0

## Lean Six Sigma Methodology

The Lean Six Sigma methodology is widely applied in industry but also in various types of business.

Aiming to achieve the best possible optimization to avoid waste and reduce failures, increasing overall quality.

This methodology is divided into 5 phases, known as **D.M.A.I.C.**:

- **D**_efine_ (Define):
  - Establish who will carry out the project;
  - Define problems objectively;
  - Focus on the most relevant and viable needs;
  - Think about the improvements that can be implemented;
- **M**_easure_ (Measure): 
  - Evaluate process performance and analyze the numbers;
  - Obtain detailed data and information about the process;
  - Find possible causes of problems;
- **A**_nalyze_ (Analyze): 
  - Think about the root causes of problems that affect the process and management;
  - Analyze the best options to correct these causes;
  - Create improvement opportunities;
- **I**_mprove_ (Improve): 
  - First test possible actions by observing the pros and cons;
  - Execute the action plan according to the needs;
  - Implement the change;
- **C**_ontrol_ (Control): 
  - Monitor changes to the established process;
  - Establish control criteria such as task lists and statistics;
  - Analyze the overall performance achieved;
  - Seek continuous process improvement;

![DMAIC](/docs/assets/business/lean-six-sigma-dmaic.png "DMAIC")

Netuno supports automation to improve the 5 phases defined in the methodology:

- **M**_easure_: Through the ability to create integrations to collect large volumes of data manually and automatically.
